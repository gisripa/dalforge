severity: error
scope: pg
title: a table, column or index name isn't a valid unquoted Postgres identifier.
---

Names must be lowercase letters, digits and `_`, start with a letter or `_`,
be at most 63 bytes (Postgres silently truncates longer names), and not be a
reserved word. dalforge never quotes identifiers, so names also work
unquoted in your custom queries.

Fix: set `(dal.v1.table).name` or `(dal.v1.field).name`. Common case: a
message called `Order` or `User` needs `name: "orders"` or `name: "users"`.
