---
severity: warning
scope: pg
title: a table has more indexes than its budget.
---

Every index adds work to every insert and to many updates. The count
includes the primary key, unique fields and derived list indexes. The
default budget is 5.

Fix: consolidate list shapes so they share indexes (see DAL206), or raise
`(dal.pg.v1.table).index_budget` if the reads justify the write cost.
