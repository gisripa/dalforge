---
severity: error
scope: core
title: a role is on a field of the wrong type, or two fields share a role.
---

| Role | Needs |
|---|---|
| `ROLE_CREATE_TIME`, `ROLE_UPDATE_TIME` | `google.protobuf.Timestamp` |
| `ROLE_DELETE_TIME` | `optional google.protobuf.Timestamp`: live rows have no deletion time |
| `ROLE_VERSION` | a required `int64` or `int32` |

Role fields can't be key fields or repeated, and each role may appear at most
once per entity.
