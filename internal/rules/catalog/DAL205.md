---
severity: error
scope: pg
title: two queries would have the same sqlc name.
---

Generated queries are named `<Store minus "Store"><Method>`. Two stores can
collide, such as `Order` and `OrderStore`, which both produce `OrderGet`, and
so can a custom query in `queries/custom/` that reuses a generated name. Each
list also generates `<Name>After`.

Fix: rename the rpc, the store, or your custom query.
