---
severity: warning
scope: core
title: a list's name doesn't say what it filters by.
---

dalforge expects `By` followed by the `eq` fields and then the range field,
CamelCased and joined with `And`, such as
`ListOrdersByAccountAndStatusAndCreatedAt`. A trailing `ID` is optional
(`ByAccount` matches `account_id`). Since each filter combination is its own
rpc, the names are how readers tell them apart.
