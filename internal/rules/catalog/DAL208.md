---
severity: error
scope: pg
title: a custom type is incomplete.
---

- A `custom_type` needs `go_type`, the Go type sqlc should use.
- `go_type` without `custom_type` does nothing.
- A known extension type (`vector`, `geometry`, `citext`, …) needs its
  extension in the file's `(dal.pg.v1.file).extensions`.
