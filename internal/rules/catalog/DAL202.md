---
severity: error
scope: pg
title: a list's `order_by` contradicts an explicit index on the same `eq` fields.
---

You declared an index such as `(author_id, created_at DESC)`, and a list on
`author_id` sorts by something else. Either the index was meant to serve the
list, in which case align them, or the list needs a second index, in which
case question whether both orders are needed.
