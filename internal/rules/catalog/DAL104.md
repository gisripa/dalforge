---
severity: error
scope: core
title: a `get.by` or `upsert.conflict_on` isn't backed by a unique constraint.
---

A get by a non-unique field could match several rows, and an upsert needs a
unique conflict target.

Fix: mark the field `unique`, declare a unique index on the fields
(`(dal.pg.v1.table).indexes` with `unique: true`), or use a list.
