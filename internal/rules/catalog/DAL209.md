---
severity: error
scope: pg
title: no Postgres type fits the field.
---

- `uint64` and `fixed64` have no Postgres type that holds their full range.
  Use `int64`, or a `custom_type` such as `numeric(20, 0)` with `go_type`.
- A `format` on a non-`string` field.
- An explicit `type` that doesn't fit the proto type (see
  [Types](types.md#overriding-the-postgres-type)).
